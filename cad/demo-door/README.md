# Demo door

A 6 inch (152.4 mm) model door in a door frame with a 2 inch (50.8 mm) deep entryway. It
stands on a table on a base plate. The hinge prints already assembled. Your white
reed switch sticks inside the doorway and its magnet sticks on the back of the door, so
the switch opens as soon as the door opens.

## What to print

Both files are in the `print` folder. Use Creality PLA on the P1S with a 0.4 mm nozzle,
0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 15 % infill, no supports and no brim. Don't move or rotate
anything on the plate.

1. `demo-door-rev2.3mf` (or `.stl`): the frame and door together, hinge already joined.
   About 125 g.
2. `demo-door-foot-rev1.3mf` (or `.stl`): the base plate. About 64 g.

## How to put it together

1. **Free the hinge.** Let the bed cool and lift the plate off. Hold the frame up off the
   table and fold the door down and round toward the frame's front (the face that was on
   the bed). The six hinge joints crack free one by one. Swing the door 10 to 20 times.
2. **Switch.** Inside the doorway, on the wall on the knob side, there is a raised
   outline. Peel the switch's tape and press it into the outline with its tall body
   toward the door. The wire comes out through the gap in one end wall. If the switch
   only fits the other way round, it is in backwards.
3. **Magnet.** On the back of the door there is a shallow pocket. Press the magnet into
   it, tall body toward the knob edge. The small notch in the pocket is on the side away
   from the body. With the door shut, the switch body and magnet body sit side by side,
   3 mm apart.
4. **Wire.** If the wire comes out of the top of the switch, run it up into the corner of
   the roof. Then tie it with a small zip tie through the two slots in the roof and lead
   it out the back. If it comes out of the bottom, use the two slots low in the same wall.
5. **Base plate.** Stand the frame in the base plate's lip. Push a zip tie down through
   each of the two holes in the doorway floor and the matching hole in the plate. Run it
   along the groove under the plate, up through the slot behind the frame and over the
   frame's back edge to its head. Pull both snug. For extra weight, put up to 8 US
   quarters in each of the two round wells at the back.
6. Optional: stick small rubber feet under the base plate so it does not slide.

## What is in the folders

`print` holds the files to print. `pictures` has previews of the door. `checks` has the
numerical test reports. `source` has the scripts that make everything, plus
`technical-notes.md` with the full details (settings, tuning, risks, licence).
`shelved\rev1` has the old version, kept for the record.

The hinge is Nick Johnson's "Clamshell Parts Box" hinge (MakerWorld 786443, CC BY-SA). This
door is shared under CC BY-SA too.
